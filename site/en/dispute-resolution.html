Dispute resolution

If you are not satisfied with how we settled a complaint, you have the right to turn to an alternative dispute resolution body. It is free of charge.

Last updated: 17 Aug 2026

1. Write to us first

Most things are settled by e-mail. Write to [TO BE COMPLETED — e-mail] and

describe what you believe is wrong. We will reply within three business days.

2. Alternative dispute resolution (ADR)

If you are a consumer and you are not satisfied with how we handled your

complaint, or if you believe we have infringed your rights, you have the right

to turn to us with a request for redress.

If we reject the request or fail to reply within 30 days, you have the right

to submit a proposal to commence alternative dispute resolution to an ADR

body under Act No. 391/2015 Coll.

The competent body is:

Slovak Trade Inspection Authority

Central Inspectorate, Department for International Relations and ADR

Bajkalská 21/A, p. o. box 29, 827 99 Bratislava 27

soi.sk · ars@soi.sk

Or another competent authorised legal entity entered in the list of ADR bodies

maintained by the Ministry of Economy of the Slovak Republic. The list is at

mhsr.sk.

You may also submit the proposal through the ADR platform available at

soi.sk/sk/alternativne-riesenie-spotrebitelskych-sporov.

3. What you should know about ADR

  • Alternative dispute resolution is free of charge for the consumer. The ADR body may charge no more than €5.
  • A proposal may be submitted within one year of the day you exercised with us the right that is the subject of the dispute.
  • ADR does not cover disputes where the quantifiable value does not exceed €20.
  • The outcome is an agreement, which is not enforceable as a court decision — the right to go to court remains.
  • ADR is not intended for businesses. If you are buying on a company ID, this article does not apply to you.
