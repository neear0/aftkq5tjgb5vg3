Shipping and payment

We dispatch on business days in neutral packaging. A lyophilised powder needs no cold chain — explained below.

Last updated: 17 Aug 2026

Dispatch

  • Orders received on a business day are dispatched the next business day.
  • Orders from Friday evening and the weekend go out on Monday.
  • We will tell you by e-mail when it has been sent, including the tracking number.

Packaging

  • Neutral packaging with no indication of the contents or the sender. There is nothing on the parcel from which the contents could be inferred.
  • The vials are fixed in place so they cannot move inside the packaging.
  • The parcel contains proof of purchase and the certificate of analysis for the batch supplied.

Why we do not ship refrigerated

People ask us this often, so we will put it plainly.

A lyophilised peptide does not need a cold chain over normal transit times.

Removing the water stabilises the substance — that is precisely why it is

lyophilised. A short-term rise in temperature during transport has no material

effect on its stability.

What matters is storage after delivery. The conditions for a given item are

on its page under the Storage tab. In practice: unreconstituted lyophilised

powder into the freezer, after reconstitution into the fridge, and protect it

from light.

The exceptions are items supplied as a solution (Lipo-C + B12, Bac Water) —

those are more sensitive and should be put away without delay after delivery.

Receiving the parcel

  • Check that the packaging is intact when you take receipt.
  • If the packaging is damaged we recommend refusing the parcel and having the damage recorded in the carrier's document.
  • If you discover the damage only after taking receipt, tell us **within 24 hours** with photographs, including the shipping label. The procedure is in the Complaints procedure.
