## 1. What we are responsible for

- That the product corresponds to the **identity and purity declared in the
  certificate of analysis** for the batch.
- That the batch number on the label matches the number on the certificate.
- That the quantity delivered corresponds to the quantity ordered.
- That the packaging was intact at the time of dispatch.

## 2. What we are not responsible for

- **Any use of the product contrary to its designation** — that is, outside
  laboratory and research use.
- Deterioration of the product through incorrect storage after receipt. Storage
  conditions are given on every product page under the Storage tab.
- Deterioration through incorrect reconstitution or handling.
- The results of experiments. We supply a substance of declared purity, not a
  result.
- Ordinary loss of quality after the shelf life has expired.

## 3. How to make a complaint

1. Write to [TO BE COMPLETED — e-mail] and give:
   - the order number,
   - the product name, strength and the **batch number from the label**,
   - a description of what does not match,
   - a photograph of the packaging and the label.
2. Within three business days we will send you an acknowledgement and instructions.
3. If the product needs to be returned we will send you the address. **Do not
   send anything without agreeing it first** — with batch-tracked goods we need
   to know what is coming.

## 4. Time limits

- We will settle a complaint **within 30 days** at the latest from the day it
  is made.
- We will inform you of the outcome in writing, that is, by e-mail.
- If the complaint is justified, you are entitled to a replacement with the same
  product, and where that is not possible, to a refund.

## 5. Complaints about a damaged parcel

- Report damage to the packaging **within 24 hours** of receipt and attach
  photographs, including the shipping label.
- If the packaging is already damaged on delivery, we recommend refusing the
  parcel and having the damage recorded in the carrier's document.
- If reported later, it may not be possible to claim the damage from the carrier.

## 6. Discrepancy with the certificate of analysis

If you have reason to believe that a product does not correspond to the declared
purity or identity:

- State this in the complaint together with the batch number.
- If you have carried out your own analysis, attach the report stating the
  method and the laboratory.
- In justified cases we will have the batch verified by an independent
  laboratory. If the discrepancy is confirmed, we bear the cost of the analysis
  and withdraw the batch from sale.
