## 3. What we do NOT process

This matters for our range:

- **We do not process health data** and we do not ask for it. Nowhere in the
  ordering process is there a field for a diagnosis, an intended use or a
  state of health.
- Health data is a special category of data under article 9 of the GDPR and
  **has no place in an order for a research substance**. If you write such
  information into a note, we will delete it.
- We do not process data about children — purchase is possible only from the
  age of 18.

## 5. Your rights

You have the right:

- of **access** to the data we hold about you;
- to **rectification** of inaccurate data;
- to **erasure**, where we no longer need the data for the purpose for which we
  obtained it (data we must keep for accounting purposes is an exception);
- to **restriction** of processing;
- to **portability** of the data in a structured format;
- to **object** to processing based on legitimate interest;
- to **withdraw consent** at any time, where processing is based on consent.

Send your request to [TO BE COMPLETED — e-mail]. We will reply within one month.

## 6. Complaints

If you believe we are processing data incorrectly, you may lodge a complaint
with the **Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic**,
Hraničná 12, 820 07 Bratislava,
[dataprotection.gov.sk](https://dataprotection.gov.sk).

## 7. Security

- Transmission is encrypted with HTTPS.
- Only people who need the data for their work have access to it.
- IP addresses recorded with the age confirmation are stored **hashed**, not in
  readable form — that is enough as proof of the confirmation and is a smaller
  intrusion into privacy.
