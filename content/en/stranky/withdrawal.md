## 1. The right to withdraw

If you are a consumer — that is, a natural person not buying in the course of
business — you have the right to withdraw from the contract **within 14 days**
without giving a reason.

The period begins on the day you or a person designated by you takes receipt of
the product. If the consignment is split into parts, the period begins with
receipt of the last part.

## 2. How to withdraw

- An unambiguous statement sent before the period expires is enough, to the
  e-mail address [TO BE COMPLETED] or by post to [TO BE COMPLETED].
- You may use the [model form](#form) at the end of this page, but you are not
  obliged to.
- The period is met if you send the statement no later than the last day of it.
- We will confirm receipt of the withdrawal without undue delay.

## 3. When withdrawal is NOT possible

This matters for our range and it is a statutory exception, not a condition of
ours.

The right of withdrawal **cannot be exercised** for a product:

- that was **sealed in protective packaging and the packaging was broken after
  delivery**, where the product is not suitable for return for reasons of
  hygiene — that is, for a **punctured or opened vial**;
- that was made to the buyer's individual specification;
- that is subject to rapid deterioration in quality.

In practice this means: **an intact, unopened vial can be returned. An opened or
punctured one cannot.** The reason is that once the packaging is broken, neither
sterility nor the identity of the contents can be guaranteed, and the product
cannot be sold again.

## 4. Returning the product

- Return the product no later than **14 days** after withdrawing.
- **The cost of return is borne by the buyer** unless we have agreed otherwise.
- Return the product in its original packaging, with the seal intact and with
  proof of purchase.
- Do not send the parcel cash on delivery — we will not accept it.

## 5. Refunds

- We will refund the money **within 14 days** of receiving the withdrawal, but
  not before the product reaches us or before you prove that you have sent it.
- We will also refund the cost of shipping, up to the amount of the cheapest
  delivery method offered. If you chose a more expensive method, the difference
  is not refunded.
- We will refund by the same means we received the payment, unless we agree
  otherwise.

## 6. Diminished value

You are liable for any diminished value of the product resulting from handling
beyond what is necessary to establish its nature and functioning. For
lyophilised reference substances this means that **inspecting the packaging and
the label is enough to establish the nature of the product** — opening the vial
is not necessary.
