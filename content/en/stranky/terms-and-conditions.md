## 3. What the products are for — read carefully

This is the most important part of these terms.

- All products in the catalog are **reference substances intended strictly for
  laboratory and research use** (*research use only*).
- **They are not medicines** under the Act on Medicines and Medical Devices, nor
  are they food supplements, cosmetic products or medical devices.
- **They are not intended for administration to humans or animals**, nor for
  the diagnosis, prevention, treatment or alleviation of any condition.
- The seller **provides no dosing, no application protocols and no health
  advice**, and will not answer questions of that kind.
- The information on the product pages is a summary of published research
  findings. **It does not constitute health claims** or a recommendation for use.

By sending an order the buyer **expressly confirms** that the products are being
acquired strictly for laboratory or research purposes and that this designation
is understood.
