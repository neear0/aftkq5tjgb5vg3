> A modified analogue of insulin-like growth factor 1.
> 83 amino acids, supplied as a lyophilised powder.

## What IGF-1 LR3 is
- Long R3 IGF-1 is a modified analogue of human IGF-1.
- It has 83 amino acids — 13 more than native IGF-1 — and an arginine at
  position 3 in place of glutamic acid.
- Both modifications are associated in the literature with a markedly longer
  biological half-life than the native molecule.
- It is not a peptide in the sense of a short chain, but a growth factor.

## Documented mechanism
- It acts on the IGF-1R receptor; published work describes activation of the
  PI3K/Akt and MAPK pathways.
- According to the literature, the substitution at position 3 reduces binding to
  the IGFBP binding proteins, which changes the availability of the molecule.
- In models, an effect on cell proliferation and differentiation is described.

## Areas of research
- IGF-1R signalling and its downstream pathways.
- Cell proliferation and growth in tissue culture.
- Glucose metabolism — IGF-1 has partial affinity for the insulin receptor.

## State of knowledge
Most of the data comes from in vitro models and cell cultures, where the LR3
variant is used precisely for its stability. Controlled clinical studies in
humans for this modified form are not available.

## Regulatory notice
IGF-1 and its analogues are growth factors with pronounced biological activity
and appear on the Prohibited List of the World Anti-Doping Agency (WADA).
This item is a **reference standard intended strictly for laboratory and
research use**. It is not a medicine and must not be used for administration to
