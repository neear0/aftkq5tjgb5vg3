## What is in this combination
KLOW is the established abbreviation for a mixture of four substances, all of
which are also available separately in our catalog:

- **GHK-Cu** — a copper tripeptide, studied in the context of collagen production.
- **BPC-157** — a pentadecapeptide, studied in models of tissue healing.
- **TB-500** — a fragment of thymosin beta-4, associated with cell migration.
- **KPV** — a tripeptide, a fragment of alpha-MSH, studied in models of inflammation.

The strength stated is the **combined mass of all four substances**. The ratio of
the individual components is given in the certificate of analysis for the batch
and differs between manufacturers.

## Documented mechanism
The combination has no mechanism of its own — the action of each component is
described in its own entry. The mixture is built on the assumption that the
substances act at different levels of healing: vessel formation, cell migration,
matrix synthesis and the inflammatory response.

## Areas of research
- Tissue healing and remodelling.
- The inflammatory response in skin.
- Comparative studies of mixtures against the individual substances.

## State of knowledge
There is literature for every component, predominantly preclinical. **For the
combination as a whole there are no controlled studies.** The claim of synergy is
a hypothesis derived from the mechanisms of the individual substances, not a finding.

With mixtures there is the further point that any observed effect cannot be
attributed to a particular component — a fundamental limitation for research use.
If the experiment needs to establish which substance is responsible for what,
the separate items are the better choice.

Anti-Doping Agency (WADA). This item is a **reference standard intended strictly
for laboratory and research use**. It is neither a medicine nor a cosmetic and we
provide no dosing or health advice with it.
