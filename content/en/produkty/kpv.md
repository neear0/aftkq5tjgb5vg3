> A tripeptide — the C-terminal fragment of alpha-MSH.
> Three amino acids (Lys-Pro-Val), supplied as a lyophilised powder.

## What KPV is
- A tripeptide of lysine, proline and valine.
- It is the C-terminal fragment of alpha-melanocyte-stimulating hormone,
  specifically its last three amino acids (11–13).
- Unlike whole alpha-MSH it **has no pigmentary effect** — it lacks the part of
  the molecule responsible for binding to MC1R.
- At three amino acids, the literature describes it as relatively stable.

## Documented mechanism
- Published work describes an effect on NF-κB signalling in models of inflammation.
- Studied in connection with transport across cell membranes via the PepT1
  transporter, which is expressed in inflamed tissue.
- In models, an effect on the production of pro-inflammatory cytokines is described.

## Areas of research
- The local inflammatory response.
- Inflammatory bowel conditions in experimental models.
- Skin inflammation and barrier function.
- Antimicrobial properties of alpha-MSH fragments.

## State of knowledge
The data come from cell cultures and animal models, chiefly mouse models of
colitis. Controlled clinical studies in humans are not available.
