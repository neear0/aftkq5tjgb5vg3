> A pentadecapeptide derived from a protein found in gastric juice.
> 15 amino acids, supplied as a lyophilised powder.

## What BPC-157 is
- A synthetic peptide of 15 amino acids — a pentadecapeptide.
- Derived from the sequence of the BPC protein present in human gastric juice.
- In research it is among the most frequently studied reparative peptides.
- The literature also refers to it as PL 14736 or Bepecin.

## Documented mechanism
- Published work describes an effect on angiogenesis in tissue healing models.
- Studied in connection with the expression of growth factors (VEGF) in
  damaged tissue.
- In animal models, an effect on the integrity of the gastrointestinal mucosa
  has been followed.
- An interaction with the nitric oxide pathway is described.

## Areas of research
- Healing of soft tissue, tendons and ligaments.
- The gastrointestinal barrier and its integrity.
- The local inflammatory response.
- Protective effects in models of toxic injury.

## State of knowledge
The overwhelming majority of the data comes from animal models, chiefly rats,
and from the work of a single research school. Controlled clinical studies in
humans are all but absent. The findings therefore cannot be transferred to use
in humans, and the sheer volume of the literature must not be mistaken for
clinical proof.
