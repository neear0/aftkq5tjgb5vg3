> A synthetic tetrapeptide derived from epithalamin.
> Four amino acids (Ala-Glu-Asp-Gly), supplied as a lyophilised powder.

## What Epithalon is
- A synthetic tetrapeptide with the sequence alanine-glutamate-aspartate-glycine.
- Derived from epithalamin, an extract of the pineal gland.
- The literature also refers to it as epitalon or the AEDG peptide.
- It belongs to the short regulatory peptides described by the Russian
  gerontological school.

## Documented mechanism
- Published work describes an effect on telomerase activity in cell cultures.
- Studied in connection with the regulation of melatonin secretion.
- In models, an interaction with gene expression through DNA binding is
  described — a mechanism referred to as peptide epigenetic regulation.

## Areas of research
- Telomerase activity and telomere length.
- Circadian regulation and melatonin.
- Cellular ageing and the proliferative potential of cells.
- Antioxidant mechanisms.

## State of knowledge
A substantial part of the literature comes from a single research environment
and from publications that are not always available in international
peer-reviewed databases. Independent replications of the key findings are rare.
Controlled clinical studies to current standards are absent. Claims of life
extension in humans are not supported.
