> Bacteriostatic water — the solvent for reconstituting lyophilised powders.
> Sterile water with 0.9 % benzyl alcohol, supplied as a solution.

## What it is
- Sterile water for injection with **0.9 % benzyl alcohol** added.
- Benzyl alcohol acts bacteriostatically — it prevents microorganisms from
  multiplying in a vial that has already been opened.
- **It is neither a peptide nor an active substance.** It is a laboratory consumable.

## What it is used for
- Reconstituting the lyophilised peptides in the catalog.
- Thanks to the bacteriostatic additive it is more suitable than plain sterile
  water wherever a vial is opened more than once.

## Why not a saline solution
- A saline solution contains no preservative, so once opened it is suitable for
  single use only.
- Some peptides are sensitive to ions; bacteriostatic water contains none.
- With copper complexes (GHK-Cu), additives in other solutions can interact with
  the metal ion.

## Handling
- Store at 20–25 °C, do not freeze.
- After the stopper is first pierced, follow the date given on the packaging.
- Wipe the stopper before every withdrawal.
- The solution must be clear and free of turbidity. Do not use it if cloudy.

## Notice
Even though this is a consumable, the same designation applies to it as to the
rest of the catalog: **strictly for laboratory and research use**. Benzyl alcohol
is not suitable for every application — for some sensitive systems, water without
a preservative is used instead.
