> Triple incretin receptor agonist (GLP-1 / GIP / glucagon).
> A peptide analogue, supplied as a lyophilised powder.

## What Reta is
- Retatrutide is a synthetic peptide analogue acting on three receptors at once.
- It combines GLP-1, GIP and glucagon receptor agonism in a single molecule.
- It belongs to the incretin analogues, a generation newer than the dual agonists.
- Supplied as a lyophilised powder for reconstitution.

## Documented mechanism
- Published work describes simultaneous activation of three receptor pathways
  involved in the regulation of energy intake and glycaemia.
- GLP-1 agonism is associated in the literature with slowed gastric emptying
  and satiety signalling.
- Engagement of the glucagon receptor is studied in connection with energy
  expenditure — this is the main difference from the dual agonists.
- How much each pathway contributes to the outcome is the subject of ongoing research.

## Areas of research
- Energy balance and the regulation of food intake.
- Glycaemic control and insulin signalling.
- Lipid metabolism.
- Comparative studies against single and dual incretin analogues.

## State of knowledge
Retatrutide is under active clinical investigation and, at the time of writing,
has not completed the full development cycle for every indication being studied.
The available data come from clinical trials run by the manufacturer and from
published literature; they do not transfer to use outside controlled research.
