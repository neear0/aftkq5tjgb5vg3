> A cyclic alpha-MSH analogue, a non-selective melanocortin receptor agonist.
> Seven amino acids in a cyclic structure, supplied as a lyophilised powder.

## What Melanotan II is
- A synthetic cyclic heptapeptide, an analogue of alpha-melanocyte-stimulating
  hormone (alpha-MSH).
- The cyclic structure makes it more resistant to degradation than linear alpha-MSH.
- It is **non-selective** — it acts on several melanocortin receptors including
  MC1R, MC3R, MC4R and MC5R. That is the main difference from Melanotan I.

## Documented mechanism
- MC1R agonism is associated in the literature with the stimulation of melanogenesis.
- Action at MC4R is studied in connection with the regulation of food intake and
  with sexual function — it is precisely this lack of selectivity that accounts
  for the broader range of effects described.
- Engagement of MC3R and MC5R is less well studied.

## Areas of research
- Melanogenesis and pigmentation.
- Melanocortin receptor signalling.
- Regulation of food intake through MC4R.

## State of knowledge
Melanotan II never completed clinical development. The published literature is
predominantly from preclinical models and case reports, not from controlled
studies. The literature also describes adverse events associated with its
non-selective action and with uncontrolled use.

## Regulatory notice
Melanotan II is not authorised as a medicine in any jurisdiction known to us,
and several medicines agencies have publicly warned against its use in humans.
This item is a **reference standard intended strictly for laboratory and
research use**. It is neither a medicine nor a cosmetic, it must not be used for
administration to humans or animals, and we provide no dosing or health advice
with it.
