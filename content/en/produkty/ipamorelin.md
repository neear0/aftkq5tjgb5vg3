> A pentapeptide — a selective agonist of the ghrelin receptor.
> Five amino acids, supplied as a lyophilised powder.

## What Ipamorelin is
- A pentapeptide with the sequence Aib-His-D-2-Nal-D-Phe-Lys.
- It belongs to the growth hormone secretagogues (GHS) — it acts on the ghrelin
  receptor, not on the GHRH receptor.
- The literature describes it as **the most selective** of the GHS group.

## Documented mechanism
- Agonism of the GHS-R receptor in the pituitary and hypothalamus.
- Published work describes that, unlike the older secretagogues (e.g. GHRP-6),
  it has no pronounced effect on the secretion of cortisol, prolactin or ACTH.
  This selectivity is its defining characteristic.
- In models it does not produce the marked increase in appetite described for
  ghrelin analogues of lower selectivity.

## Areas of research
- Selectivity of growth hormone secretagogues.
- The GH – IGF-1 axis.
- Comparative studies with GHRP-2 and GHRP-6.
- Gastrointestinal motility — GHS-R is expressed there as well.

## State of knowledge
Ipamorelin went through early phases of clinical development, which were not
completed. The available data are from preclinical models and from limited
clinical data.

## Regulatory notice
Growth hormone secretagogues appear on the Prohibited List of the World
Anti-Doping Agency (WADA). This item is a **reference standard intended strictly
for laboratory and research use**. It is not a medicine and we provide no dosing
or health advice with it.
