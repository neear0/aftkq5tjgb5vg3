> A linear alpha-MSH analogue with selectivity for the MC1R receptor.
> 13 amino acids, supplied as a lyophilised powder.

## What Melanotan I is
- A synthetic linear tridecapeptide, an analogue of alpha-MSH. The literature
  also refers to it as afamelanotide.
- Unlike Melanotan II it is **more selective for MC1R**, the receptor associated
  with pigmentation.
- Greater selectivity means, in the literature, a narrower range of systemic
  effects described than for the non-selective analogue.

## Documented mechanism
- MC1R agonism on melanocytes is associated with the stimulation of eumelanin
  production.
- Published work describes the photoprotective significance of eumelanin against
  UV radiation.
- Action at MC3R–MC5R is substantially weaker than that of Melanotan II.

## Areas of research
- Melanogenesis and melanin types.
- Photoprotection and the response of skin to UV load.
- Melanocortin receptor selectivity — comparative studies with MT-II.

## State of knowledge
Unlike Melanotan II, afamelanotide has completed clinical development and holds
authorisation in some jurisdictions for a very narrow indication. Those clinical
data apply to an authorised medicine given under medical supervision and are not
transferable to other uses.
