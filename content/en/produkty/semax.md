> A synthetic heptapeptide derived from an ACTH fragment.
> Seven amino acids, supplied as a lyophilised powder.

## What Semax is
- A heptapeptide — the ACTH (4–7) fragment with an attached Pro-Gly-Pro sequence
  that stabilises the molecule.
- Deliberately built so that it **has none of the hormonal activity of ACTH**,
  that is, it does not stimulate the adrenal cortex.
- Developed in Russia, where it is authorised as a medicine for some indications.

## Documented mechanism
- Published work describes an effect on the expression of neurotrophic factors,
  particularly BDNF and NGF.
- Studied in connection with a modulatory action on dopaminergic and
  serotonergic signalling.
- In models, a neuroprotective effect in ischaemic injury is described.
- The Pro-Gly-Pro triad at the end is, according to the literature, the reason
  for its relatively good stability compared with other short peptides.

## Areas of research
- Neurotrophic factors and neuroplasticity.
- Neuroprotection in models of ischaemia.
- Cognitive function and attention in animal models.
- The stress response.

## State of knowledge
Semax has an extensive literature, but predominantly Russian and not always
available in international peer-reviewed databases. Independent replications by
Western institutions are rare. Authorisation in one jurisdiction does not mean
efficacy demonstrated to the standards required by the EMA or the FDA.
