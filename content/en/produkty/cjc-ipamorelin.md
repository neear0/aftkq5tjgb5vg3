> A combined formulation of a GHRH analogue and a selective secretagogue.
> CJC-1295 and Ipamorelin in one vial, supplied as a lyophilised powder.

## What is in this combination
- **CJC-1295** — a modified 29-amino-acid GHRH analogue (the GRF 1-29 fragment)
  with four substitutions that protect it from degradation.
- **Ipamorelin** — a pentapeptide, a selective agonist of the ghrelin receptor (GHS-R).
- The strength stated is the **combined mass of both substances**; the ratio is
  in the certificate of analysis for the batch.
- Watch out for the mix-up: the version without DAC (Drug Affinity Complex) has a
  markedly shorter half-life than CJC-1295 with DAC. Which one is in the batch is
  stated in the certificate.

## Documented mechanism
- CJC-1295 acts on GHRH receptors in the pituitary.
- Ipamorelin acts on a different receptor — GHS-R — and is described in the
  literature as selective, that is, without the more pronounced effect on
  cortisol and prolactin that the older secretagogues have.
- The combination is studied because the substances act **on two different
  pathways of the same axis**. Published work describes an additive effect in models.

## Areas of research
- The GHRH – GH – IGF-1 axis and its pulsatile regulation.
- Secretagogue selectivity and effects on other pituitary hormones.
- Comparative studies of the individual substances against their combination.

## State of knowledge
The data come from preclinical models and early clinical phases for the
individual substances. The combination itself has no controlled studies in humans.

intended strictly for laboratory and research use**. It is not a medicine and we
