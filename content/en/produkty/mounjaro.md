> Dual incretin receptor agonist (GIP / GLP-1).
> A 39-amino-acid peptide analogue, supplied as a lyophilised powder.

## What it is
- Tirzepatide is a synthetic peptide of 39 amino acids.
- It acts as an agonist at two receptors at once: GIP and GLP-1.
- The molecule carries a fatty acid attached to the chain, which the literature
  links to an extended duration of action.
- Supplied as a lyophilised powder for reconstitution.

## Documented mechanism
- Published work describes an additive effect of simultaneous GIP and GLP-1
  receptor activation compared with GLP-1 agonism alone.
- GLP-1 agonism is associated with satiety signalling and with slowed gastric
  emptying.
- The role of the GIP pathway in lipid metabolism and in the insulin response
  is the subject of further investigation.

## Areas of research
- Glycaemic control and insulin secretion.
- Energy balance and the regulation of food intake.
- Lipid metabolism and the distribution of fat mass.

## State of knowledge
Tirzepatide has an extensive clinical literature, including controlled trials.
That changes nothing about what this item is for: data from clinical trials
apply to an authorised medicine given under medical supervision, not to a
reference substance in a laboratory.
