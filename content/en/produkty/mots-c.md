> A mitochondrial peptide associated with energy metabolism.
> 16 amino acids, supplied as a lyophilised powder.

## What MOTS-C is
- MOTS-c is a 16-amino-acid mitochondrial peptide.
- It is encoded in the gene for mitochondrial 12S rRNA — not in nuclear DNA.
- It belongs to the mitochondrial-derived peptides (MDPs).
- It takes part in communication between the mitochondrion and the nucleus.

## Documented mechanism
- In models it activates the folate → AICAR → AMPK pathway.
- Published work describes an effect on glucose tolerance and insulin sensitivity.
- Followed in connection with the use of energy substrates and with metabolic
  flexibility.
- In experimental models, an effect on the response to oxidative stress is described.

## Relation to physical exertion
- Physical exertion is described as a factor influencing MOTS-c levels.
- It is listed among the mitochondrial signals associated with adaptation to exercise.
- In experimental models, an effect on endurance performance has been followed.

## Studied in combination
- Published work looks at its interplay with AMPK activation.
- Studied alongside substances acting on mitochondrial function (e.g. NR, resveratrol).
- Related items in the catalog: NAD+, 5-Amino-1MQ.

## Areas of research
- Energy metabolism and mitochondrial function.
- Insulin signalling and glucose homeostasis.
- Adaptation to physical exertion.
- Cellular ageing.

## State of knowledge
Most of the available data comes from in vitro models and animal studies.
Controlled clinical studies in humans are limited, so the findings cannot be
transferred to use in humans.
