> A hexapeptide — one of the first growth hormone secretagogues described.
> Six amino acids, supplied as a lyophilised powder.

## What GHRP-6 is
- A hexapeptide with the sequence His-D-Trp-Ala-Trp-D-Phe-Lys.
- Historically the first of the GHRP group; its discovery led to the
  identification of the ghrelin receptor even before ghrelin itself was found.
- Unlike Ipamorelin it is **non-selective**.

## Documented mechanism
- Agonism of the GHS-R receptor.
- Published work describes that, besides growth hormone, it also affects the
  secretion of cortisol, prolactin and ACTH. That is the main difference from
  the newer selective secretagogues.
- In models a pronounced effect on appetite is described — a mechanism shared
  with ghrelin.

## Areas of research
- Historical research on the GH axis and the discovery of the ghrelin receptor.
- Regulation of food intake.
- Cytoprotective effects described in models of tissue injury.
- Comparative pharmacology of the secretagogues.

## State of knowledge
GHRP-6 has the longest literature of the whole group — it has been used in
research since the 1980s. Clinical development as a medicine was never
completed, however, and newer selective substances have largely replaced it
in research.
